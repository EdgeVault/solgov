# Roadmap

Direction notes, not commitments. Reflects current focus areas.

## Short-term

- Continued protocol coverage. Community-suggested additions vetted on-chain before landing.
- Weekly digest broadcast and daily digest reliability. Severity-tier routing kept tight so personal subscriptions stay useful.
- Contrast and mobile polish on the dashboard for first-time visitors.

## Medium-term

- Deeper signer-funder detection. Expand the cross-protocol funder registry with more on-chain signal.
- Blast Radius enhancements. Clearer cascade scoring and dependency mapping across protocol versus asset nodes.
- Non-Squads governance coverage. Cleaner support for Realms DAO entries and appchain-style protocols once their mainnet surfaces are published.

## Long-term

- Stay focused on the governance and operational layer that audits and formal verification do not cover. The continuous monitoring layer between deep assessments.
- Keep solgov free and public. Open source, no token, no auth on the API.
- Support protocol teams who want the live drift-detection layer alongside their own audits.
